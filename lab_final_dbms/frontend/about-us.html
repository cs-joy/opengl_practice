About KeyNest

KeyNest is a property management platform that connects renters with quality homes across Bangladesh. Browse listings, view details and photos, and request bookings online — our team confirms every request through the admin dashboard.

This project is a BSc Computer Science & Engineering lab MVP: a public client portal (vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript) with a separate secured admin panel and MySQL backend.
